# Submission Template

Copy this file into a new entry (e.g. `marketplace-hub/entries/your-entry-name.md`) and fill it in. Keep it short — the goal is enough context for someone else to understand and reuse what you built, not a full writeup.

## Title

One line: what is this?

## Problem

What manual or repetitive task does this replace or speed up?

## What it is

Agent, prompt, workflow, script, or connector? What tools or platforms does it depend on?

## Impact

Rough sense of time saved, quality improved, or process it replaced. Estimates are fine — cite the source if you have one (e.g. "saves ~2 hrs/week on the Q-close report").

## How to reuse it

Steps someone else would take to adapt this to their own task.

## Owner

Your name, so people can ask questions.

## Category / tags

Function (reporting, triage, research, content generation, …) and team or business area.
